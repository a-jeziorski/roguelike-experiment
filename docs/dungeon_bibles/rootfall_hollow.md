# Rootfall Hollow — Dungeon Bible

*Brief by design, the fifth of a short run of small dungeons exercising
`tools/procgen/` against real, shipped content, one per algorithm, per
`docs/region_bibles/dust_reach.md`'s plan.*

## What it is

A single-level dungeon (`data/dungeons/rootfall_hollow/levels/level_01.lvl`,
60x40) generated by `tools/procgen/dla.py`'s diffusion-limited
aggregation: a single seed at the grid's center, grown outward by random
walkers sticking wherever they first touch the existing structure. The
result is an irregular, asymmetric hollow that branches and thickens the
way roots, cracks, or mineral veins actually grow - visually distinct
from both the smooth, rounded blobs cellular automata produces and
Voronoi's walled, room-shaped chambers (see `docs/procgen_algorithms.md`'s
DLA section for the generator itself).

**Era**: Natural, no era or faction - the same category `silver_mountain_caves`
belongs to (see `docs/world_history.md`'s roster). Nothing built this;
nothing needs to have built this. It's just ground that opened up this
way.

## Roster

None. Zero entities, zero items - the point of this pass is exercising
the generator and the content pipeline, not a combat encounter.

## Layout notes

- `player_start` and the single terminal `stairs_down` sit at the two
  floor tiles `tools.procgen.base.farthest_pair` found furthest apart (by
  8-directional graph distance) within the level's largest connected
  component.
- No `next_level` - reaching the stairs leaves the dungeon and returns
  directly to Dust Reach, the same terminal-stairway pattern
  `fallen_colonnade` and `cloven_warren` both use.
- Grown at a larger footprint (60x40, vs. 45x30 for this run's other test
  dungeons) specifically so the branching character DLA produces has room
  to actually read as branching rather than saturating into a dense blob
  - see the algorithm's own doc section for why grid size matters here
    more than it does for the others.

## Tone notes

- Keep this empty and unexplained, the same restraint `silver_mountain_caves`
  extends to its own natural origin - there's no mystery to solve here,
  just terrain.
- If a future pass wants to give this hollow actual inhabitants (the way
  Silver Mountain Caves has its own wildlife), that's a deliberate
  follow-up, not something this document already assumes.
