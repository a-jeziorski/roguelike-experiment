# The Cloven Warren — Dungeon Bible

*Brief by design, the third of a short run of small dungeons exercising
`tools/procgen/` against real, shipped content, one per algorithm, per
`docs/region_bibles/dust_reach.md`'s plan.*

## What it is

A single-level dungeon (`data/dungeons/cloven_warren/levels/level_01.lvl`,
45x30) generated by `tools/procgen/voronoi_regions.py`: nine seed points
scattered across the grid, every cell assigned to its nearest seed (a
raster Voronoi diagram), then a one-tile wall margin eroded around every
region boundary. What survives reads as a warren of small, irregularly-
shaped chambers, each walled off from its neighbors, connected by narrow
corridors wherever two regions actually touch (see
`docs/procgen_algorithms.md`'s Voronoi section for the generator itself).

**Era**: The Elder Age. Unlike Forgotten Ruins or the Elder Cairn, this
site has no excavation, no guardian, nothing built *on top of* an obvious
older structure - just the partition itself, regular enough that it can't
be natural but too old and strange for anyone to say who made it. That's
deliberate: per `docs/world_history.md`'s standing rule, the Elder Age
stays vague after the fact, and a Voronoi cell layout's own texture -
organic-looking boundaries that are nonetheless too evenly divided to be
a real cave - is a good mechanical match for "a shape too deliberate to be
natural."

## Roster

None. Zero entities, zero items - the point of this pass is exercising the
generator and the content pipeline, not a combat encounter.

## Layout notes

- `player_start` and the single terminal `stairs_down` sit at the two
  floor tiles `tools.procgen.base.farthest_pair` found furthest apart (by
  8-directional graph distance) within the level's largest connected
  component, the same placement discipline every other procgen-sourced
  level in this project uses.
- Every pair of regions whose raster cells actually touch got a corridor
  between their seed points during generation - the whole warren is
  guaranteed one connected graph, never isolated chambers a player
  couldn't reach.
- No `next_level` - reaching the stairs leaves the dungeon and returns
  directly to Dust Reach, the same terminal-stairway pattern
  `fallen_colonnade`'s single level uses.

## Tone notes

- Keep the Elder Age's usual restraint: the description above already
  says as much as this site should ever say. Don't retroactively explain
  who partitioned it or why in a later pass without a real reason to.
- If a future pass wants to give this site an actual guardian or purpose
  (the way the Elder Cairn or Forgotten Ruins have one), that's a
  deliberate follow-up, not something this document already assumes.
